Animals

Ape

It is a large primate that lacks a tail, including the gorilla, chimpanzees, orangutan, and gibbons.

Dog

It is a domesticated carnivorous mammal that typically has a long snout, an acute sense of smell, nonretractable claws, and a barking, howling, or whining voice.

Jaguar

It is a large heavily built cat that has a yellowish-brown coat with black spots, found mainly in the dense forests of Central and South America.

Mouse

A mouse is a small rodent. Characteristically, mice are known to have a pointed snout, small rounded ears, a body-length scaly tail, and a high breeding rate.

Countries

Australia

A large island country and continent in the south-west Pacific Ocean. Its capital city is Canberra and its official language is English.

Denmark

Officially the Kingdom of Denmark, a Nordic country in Northern Europe. It is the furthest south of the Scandinavian countries, bordering Germany to the south.

Japan

Japan is an island country in East Asia, in the northwest Pacific Ocean, bordered on the west by the Sea of Japan, extending from the Sea of Okhotsk in the north.

Myanmar

Officially the Republic of the Union of Myanmar, also known as Burma, is the largest country by area in Mainland Southeast Asia with a population of about 55 million.

Foods

Almonds

The almond is a species of small tree from the genus Prunus, cultivated worldwide for its seed, a culinary nut, classified in the subgenus Amygdalus.

Dragon Fruit

A large oval to oblong fruit of several cacti with leathery skin with prominent scaly spikes and juicy flesh. Widely cultivated in Mexico and Central America.

Jelly

A sweet, clear, semisolid spread or preserve made from fruit juice and sugar boiled to a thick consistency.

Marshmallow

A confectionery made from sugar, water, and gelatin whipped to a solid-but-soft consistency. Used as a filling in baking or molded into shapes coated with corn starch.

Months

January

The first month of the year, 31 days long. Known as New Year's Day on the 1st. On average the coldest month in the Northern Hemisphere and warmest in the Southern.

March

The third month of the year in the Gregorian calendar. It marks the first month of spring in the Northern Hemisphere and the first month of autumn in the Southern Hemisphere.

August

Originally the sixth month of the ancient Roman calendar known as Sextilis, meaning "sixth month" in Latin, before the calendar was reformed.

December

The twelfth month of the year. From Latin December, literally "tenth month," derived from decem meaning "ten" — related to decimal and dime.
